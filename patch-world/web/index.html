Creatures with Cadence brains forage, get poisoned, learn and evolve while you watch.

Berries nourish in summer and sicken in winter, and nothing a creature senses says which season it is. Each learner is one continuing Cadence brain that learns from what its own actions bring; a poisoning surprises it, and the surprise wakes its learning. Beside the learners live two controls: frozen twins, the same brains that never learn, and random walkers. Flip the season and watch who keeps eating the berries.

    Every light is this creature's real state this moment. Neurons light with the activation they settle to, played sweep by sweep through the settle its action came from; tracts glow with the signal their synapses carry (warm excitatory, cool inhibitory) and flash white where this moment's lesson changed synapses; the dopamine nucleus washes gold or blue with the critic's prediction error; the locus coeruleus glows with arousal, and the brain brightens while it learns; hippocampal slots light when the recall memory reads them, threaded to the places they remember. Regions are named for the role their population plays. Hover a region for what it is.
