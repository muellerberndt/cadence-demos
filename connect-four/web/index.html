cadence/connect four Starting the brain…

Play Connect Four against a Cadence brain and watch every neuron settle on its move.

Take turns dropping stones; four in a row wins. The brain sees the stones and the cells where a stone would land. On its turn the whole brain settles once: its column neurons propose the move, and three more hold its verdict on the position. With Brain + search a look-ahead over the rules vets the proposal; Brain alone plays the strongest column neuron.

Motor neuronsone per column
What it imagines you would reply

Before it moves, the brain privately settles on the board each column would leave and reads the reply it would make in your seat (Brain.imagine). Its move comes from one settle on the real board; it does not search these. Off by default: imagining settles seven boards before each move, which adds several seconds in the browser.
