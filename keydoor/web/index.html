Cadence key door

One continuing Cadence creature walks a corridor in this page: floor, a chest, a lamp, a few levers, a door. The key is in the chest; the door pays when it is opened with the key, and touching anything without one costs. The creature was raised through 500 trips and arrives mid-life, fed and calm: it answers from one settle and learns nothing. Move the key to the lamp and watch it starve, rouse, search at a cost, and settle again. The brain learns here, in your browser.

Routine: a calm creature answers with the greedy choice of one qualified settle and changes nothing. Aroused: a want (its need unmet) or a surprise (a contradicted forecast) makes it sample, keep eligibility, learn from every outcome and write memory. The need, the threshold, the heat and every other constant of that law are genes; this page runs the founders with the chamber's need of 0.03 reward per moment.
