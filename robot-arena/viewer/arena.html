Brain inspector

Click a robot in the ring or in the standings to watch its brain: what it senses, how its association cortex settled, what its motor cortex chose, and how aroused it is. Every value is read from the replay, moment by moment.

Every robot is one continuing Cadence brain wired to each of its motors. Blue means it answers from a calm, settled state and learns nothing; orange means something surprised it or it wants more, so it explores and learns. The ring closes; outside it, robots burn.
