Cadence eyes

One Cadence brain runs in this page and follows every tracked shape. Each tracked shape has an eye on the pixels below. Every frame the brain answers, for each eye, where its shape is, and the eye jumps there. The pixels are all the brain gets. While nothing moves it stays in equilibrium and does no settling at all.
