What runs here. Each game is an Atari 2600 emulated in a worker. Its brain is born in a second worker with a fixed seed: nine cortical columns over the 84 × 84 retina, a reflection ring, a policy ring and a value nucleus, 61,000 relations in one settling organ. It watches a scripted teacher (Freeway: always up; Atlantis: always fire), admits what it sees in batches, takes the controls once its own decoded actions agree with the teacher often enough, and then learns from every reward. The brain's engine is a JavaScript port of the Cadence 0.50.0 reference engine, checked against the library on recorded fixtures; the emulator is the 6502.ts core. Source, parity harness and receipts.
